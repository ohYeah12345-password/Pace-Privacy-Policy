# Project Pace — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

Project Pace ("Pace", "the app") is a fitness, planning, reading and games app. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

## The short version
- Most of your data **never leaves your phone**: workouts, routines, planner, journal, water, books and game saves are stored only on your device.
- If you **create an account**, a small amount of data is stored on our server so friends, XP, achievements and leaderboards work.
- Your **journal is never uploaded or shared**.
- There are **no ads**, **no analytics or tracking SDKs**, and we **never sell** your data.

## Data stored only on your device
Workouts and sets, routines and programs, planner items, journal entries, water intake, library items and reading progress, downloaded books, imported comics and photos you choose as backgrounds or icons, saved websites, arcade saves and scores, music links, and app settings. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Data stored on our server (only if you create an account)
| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Email address and password (handled by our auth provider; passwords are never visible to us) | Sign-in, email confirmation, password reset |
| Google account name and email (only if you choose "Continue with Google") | Sign-in |
| Username, display name, avatar choice, bio, privacy settings | Your profile |
| Friend requests, friends, blocks and reports you make | Friends and safety features |
| Activity events: that you finished a workout, completed a daily challenge, set an arcade score, read for a period or finished a book, with the time | XP, levels, achievements, leaderboards and your friends' activity feed |

Activity events are kept small: for a workout, its name, duration and number of sets; for games, the game and score; for reading, the minutes read. They never include your exercises, weights lifted, body measurements or journal. Leaderboards never use body weight, calories or appearance.

You control who sees your profile and activity (everyone, friends only, or only you) in **Settings → Account → Privacy**.

## Services the app connects to
- **Supabase** (database and authentication hosting) — stores the account data above.
- **Google** — only if you choose Google sign-in.
- **Open Library, MangaDex, AniList** — when you search for books, manga and comics, or open recommendations, your search text and the titles in your library that recommendations are based on are sent to these services. Manga cover pictures are loaded from AniList, book covers from Open Library. No account data is sent.
- **Project Gutenberg mirrors and the Internet Archive** — when you download a free book or open a free comic, the request for that file is sent to them.
- **Web reader** — if you add a website in the Web reader, your phone loads that site directly, like any browser. That site sees your device and can set its own cookies, and it is governed by its own privacy policy. Pace does not send what you read there to us. The sites you save, your place on each, your custom names and icons, and a short list of recently visited sites are stored only on your phone.
- **YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify** — only opened when you tap a link; they have their own privacy policies.

## Notifications
Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent to our server.

## Deleting your data
- **In the app:** Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, friends, activity, achievements and sign-in account from our server.
- **Without the app:** email us at **dschm63@hotmail.com** from the address you signed up with, asking us to delete your Pace account. We delete it within 30 days.
- On-device data is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Children
Pace is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Security
Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). On your phone, your sign-in session is encrypted with a key held in the device's secure storage. On the server, every table is protected so people can only read what you have chosen to share.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
